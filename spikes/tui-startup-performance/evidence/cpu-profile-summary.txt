TUI startup CPU profile summary
================================

Scope
-----
These are share-safe analyzer results derived from two source/dev Bun CPU
profiles. Raw profiles and Bun Markdown reports are intentionally not tracked:
they contain absolute user paths, are multi-megabyte artifacts, include shutdown
after Ctrl-C, and are not production/compiled hotspot profiles.

Metric
------
The repository analyzer reports leaf sample/hit counts, not Bun Markdown
self-time. Percentages below are shares of selected leaf samples. Inclusive
product frames count samples whose ancestor chain includes that product frame;
they are not additive. Dependency names retain the versions recorded by the
profile, but all paths have been reduced to repository-relative paths.

Interpretation limits
---------------------
- The five listed Babel buckets total 32.33% of controlled leaf samples and
  31.71% of pure leaf samples. This is development transform/loader work, not
  evidence that production TUI code has the same distribution.
- Bun Markdown attributed 1.08 s and 10.84 s self-time to single leaves in the
  controlled and pure reports. The corresponding maximum async deltas were
  1080.6 ms and 10841.1 ms, so those self-time values are not reliable causal
  hotspot measurements.
- The compiled executable ignored BUN_OPTIONS=--cpu-prof during the audit.
  There is no compiled JavaScript/source profile. macOS native samples were
  exploratory and mostly stripped/native frames, so they are not included.
- Hit counts localize sampled activity but do not by themselves prove what
  controls process-to-prompt wall time.
