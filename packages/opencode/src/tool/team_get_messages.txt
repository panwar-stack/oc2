Read pending messages addressed to the current team session.
Use this when coordinating with teammates, reading a teammate result, or checking the team mailbox.
Messages are marked delivered after this tool returns them.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT call this repeatedly in a loop waiting for messages. When you or a teammate calls team_send_message, team_broadcast, or team_plan_submit, the recipient session is automatically woken and new messages are injected as a <team-messages> block on the next loop iteration. Check messages once at a real coordination boundary. If the mailbox is empty, do not poll.

For the active team lead: Continue useful decomposition, integration, review, or decision work. When no useful work remains, finish the current response normally. The runtime parks successful finalization while finite teammates remain active. Do not sleep, repeatedly read team state, ask for routine updates, or send filler. Teammates must send material progress, blockers, questions, and results without a lead status request. Relevant teammate or user events wake the lead. Teammates may be busy inside their current run and will process new messages only at their next prompt boundary.

Remember: your role is to coordinate and integrate teammate results. Do NOT take over a teammate's active task. Delegate, then finish normally when no useful coordination work remains. Trust your teammates to produce results. When results arrive, evaluate whether the findings reveal new sub-tasks that can be delegated to new or existing teammates.
